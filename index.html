📢 OFFICIAL PLAYOFF QUALIFICATION RULES

The qualification roadmap from the group stages to the trophy.

1️⃣ GROUP STAGE — TOP 3 CUTOFF

  • Only the Top 3 teams from Group A, Group B and Group C advance to the Playoff Pool — 9 teams total.
  • Group ranking order:
    1. Win Points
    2. Net Card Differential (NCD)
    3. Head-to-Head

2️⃣ THE 9TH-TEAM ELIMINATION — NCD BUBBLE

  • The 9 qualified teams are combined into a single cross-group leaderboard.
  • Top 8 teams advance to the Quarterfinals (Seeds #1 through #8).
  • The #9 ranked team is ELIMINATED.
⚠️ NCD Formula Reminder
NCD = (Opponent Card Points − Own Card Points) ÷ Total Rounds
Keep your remaining card penalty as low as possible in every round — every single card counts!
